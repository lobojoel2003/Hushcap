# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in the Hushcap community
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, deafness or hearing status, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Hushcap is built for and with Deaf and hard-of-hearing people. We value lived experience, and we
communicate in writing so everyone can take part equally.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences and communication styles;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the people who rely on captions;
- using accessible language and providing text alternatives for images and video.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, derogatory comments or personal attacks;
- mocking anyone's disability, hearing status or communication needs;
- publishing others' private information, including recordings, without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this
Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting channel). All reports will be reviewed promptly
and confidentially.
